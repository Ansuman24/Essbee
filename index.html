TallyPrime Overview

What TallyPrime Does For You

📒 Simple Accounting
  • Pre-defined accounting groups & chart of accounts
  • Groups & ledgers management
  • Multi-currency & multiple companies
  • Group company consolidation
  • Post-dated transactions
🛒 Purchase & Sales
  • GST compliant invoice (Tax Invoice, Bill of Supply)
  • Multiple billing formats & price lists
  • Sales & purchase order processing
  • Complete GRN and delivery note tracking
📦 Inventory Management
  • Unlimited stock groups & batch management
  • Multi-location / godown support
  • Job costing & item cost tracking
  • Multiple stock valuation methods
🏛️ Statutory & GST
  • Manage complete GST compliance
  • Filing GSTR 1 & 3B in JSON & Excel
  • Annual GST computation for GSTR 9
  • Generate e-Way bills (single & consolidated)
  • TDS & TCS management
📊 Business Reports
  • Balance Sheet, P&L, Trial Balance
  • Ledger reports, Cash/Bank books
  • Stock summary & ageing analysis
  • Cost centre & budget tracking
  • Ratio analysis & comparative reports
🏦 Banking
  • Auto bank reconciliation (Auto BRS)
  • Post-dated cheque management
  • Payment advice & deposit slip
🌐 Internet & Remote Access
  • Secure remote access from anywhere
  • E-mailing of invoices & reports
  • License & user management online
🔒 Data Security
  • Data backup & restore
  • Multiple user security controls
  • Password policy management
  • Tally Vault & Tally Audit
🏭 Manufacturing
  • Multi Bill of Material (BOM)
  • Manufacturing journal
  • Job order & job work processing
  • Export reports: Excel, PDF, HTML, XML
